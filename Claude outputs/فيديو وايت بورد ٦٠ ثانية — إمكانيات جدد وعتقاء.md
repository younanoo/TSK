## ٢. الـ Prompt (لأداة إنتاج الفيديو)

الـ prompt بالإنجليزية لأن أدوات الفيديو تفهمها أفضل. أدوات الذكاء الاصطناعي ما زالت تكتب العربية بشكل مكسور، فلا تطلب منها كتابة نصوص عربية: اترك أماكن النصوص فارغة، وأضف النصوص العربية من عمود «النص المكتوب على الشاشة» في برنامج المونتاج (VideoScribe أو CapCut).

```
Create a 60-second whiteboard animation explainer video, 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps.

STYLE
- Clean white background, a realistic right hand holding a black marker draws every element live (classic whiteboard / VideoScribe style).
- Line art: black marker strokes, minimal, friendly, flat. Only two accent colors used sparingly as marker fills: deep navy #0F3355 and warm gold #C9A227.
- Simple, modest characters (no detailed faces). No religious figures depicted, no icons of Jesus or saints. Bible shown as a closed book.
- Real website screenshots appear inside hand-drawn device frames (laptop, phone, projector screen). The hand slides each screenshot in, then circles or underlines the important part with the gold marker.
- Smooth camera pans between scenes on one continuous whiteboard; no hard cuts.
- IMPORTANT: Do NOT generate any written text or letters in the drawings. Leave a clean empty area in each scene (top or bottom third) for Arabic captions that will be added later in the editor. Layout is right-to-left: place the main subject on the right, the empty caption area on the left or bottom.

AUDIO
- A separate Arabic voice-over will be added. Pace the scenes to the timings below.
- Soft, light acoustic background music at low volume, plus subtle marker-squeak sound effects.

SCENES
1 (0-5s): A confused person sitting in front of a tall stack of books; question marks above their head.
2 (5-10s): The hand draws a laptop; screenshot 01 (website home page) slides into the laptop screen. A small sparkle around it.
3 (10-17s): Screenshot 02 (chapter page) in the laptop; gold circle around a verse number; an arrow leads to screenshot 03 (cross-references panel) which pops out larger.
4 (17-25s): Screenshot 04 (word meaning popup), gold circle on the Greek word; then screenshot 05 (dictionaries). The hand stamps a big empty badge shape (caption added later).
5 (25-31s): Screenshot 06 (study mode with numbers above words), then screenshot 07 (three translations side by side) with arrows linking the three columns.
6 (31-36s): An empty speech bubble, an arrow splitting into two branches pointing to two small scrolls (Greek and Hebrew); then screenshot 08 (search page).
7 (36-42s): Screenshot 09 (mind map network) grows from the center; then the hand draws a simple ancient Mediterranean map with a dotted travel route between three cities, marked with pins.
8 (42-48s): Screenshot 10 (reading plan) on a phone frame; then a horizontal timeline arrow with dots appears, and screenshot 11 slides above it.
9 (48-53s): A projector drawn on a table beams screenshot 12 (large verse on black slide) onto a big screen in front of rows of chairs.
10 (53-60s): Three big gold check marks drawn one by one in a vertical list; then an empty banner for the website address and the site logo in the center. Hold the final frame for 2 seconds.
```

## ٤. نصائح الإنتاج

- **الأسهل والأدق:** VideoScribe أو Doodly: ارفع الصور، واجعل اليد «ترسمها» أو تسحبها، واكتب النصوص العربية بخط Cairo أو Tajawal.
- **أدوات الذكاء الاصطناعي** (مثل Veo أو Kling): استخدم الـ prompt مشهدًا مشهدًا (٥–٨ ثوانٍ لكل مقطع)، ثم ركّب الصور والنصوص فوقها في CapCut.
- **لفيسبوك:** أضف ترجمة نصية (captions) بالعربية لأن أغلب المشاهدين يشاهدون بلا صوت، واعمل نسخة ٩:١٦ للريلز بنفس السكريبت.
